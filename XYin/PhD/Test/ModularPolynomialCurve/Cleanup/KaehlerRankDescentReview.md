# Differential stalk-rank descent

For a scalar tower R→A→B, a faithfully flat map lifts every prime of A to B.
Formal etaleness identifies relative differentials with base change; their
local ranks agree at those primes. Thus constant rank on B descends to A.
The primary result uses only finite/flat Ω[A/R], formal etaleness of A→B,
and genuine faithful flatness. It does not assume smoothness, Noetherian or
domain rings, global standard smoothness, or a finite extension.
