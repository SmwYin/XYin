## API and mathematical audit

- `Matrix.IsColumnHermiteNormalForm` is a reusable `Prop` structure indexed by any preorder, with
  named fields for triangularity, positive pivots, and reduced upper entries.
- `Module.Basis.HermiteNormalForm` deliberately parallels Smith normal form: it stores a basis of
  the submodule, the inclusion matrix relative to a fixed ambient basis, the `toMatrix` identity,
  and the HNF certificate.
- For a nonsingular matrix `A`, the bottom-row gcd and Bézout coefficients give a unimodular right
  factor eliminating the lower-left entry.  A sign correction makes the first pivot positive,
  and Euclidean remainder reduction puts the upper-right entry in `[0, d)`.
- `Matrix.exists_right_mul_isColumnHermiteNormalForm` exposes this useful rank-two matrix lemma.
  `Matrix.toLinearEquiv` and `Basis.map` then transport the column operation to a new basis of the
  original submodule.
- The determinant of the initial inclusion matrix is nonzero because the corresponding endomorphism
  is injective; this avoids any quotient-finiteness assumption in the existence proof.
